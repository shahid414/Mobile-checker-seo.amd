What Is Mobile-First Indexing?

Mobile-first indexing means Google uses the mobile version of a page's content for indexing and ranking — not the desktop version. Here is how it works, how it rolled out, and what to check on your own site.

The timeline

Google started mobile-first indexing in 2016, made it the default for new sites in July 2019, and declared the switch complete in October 2023. Then, after July 5, 2024, it started crawling the remaining sites with Googlebot Smartphone only. Today there is effectively one crawler that matters: the smartphone one.

Search Console removed the old “Indexing crawler” line in November 2023 because it no longer told you anything — every site is crawled as mobile now.

What Google actually checks

Google's own best practices boil down to parity: the same content and the same clear headings on mobile and desktop, equivalent titles and meta descriptions, the same robots meta tags, the same structured data on both versions, lazy loading that does not wait for a swipe or click, and images with the same alt text.

With responsive design these conditions usually hold on their own, because both devices get the same HTML. The risk lies in dynamic serving, separate mobile URLs and themes that trim content for phones.

How to check your own site

Use our mobile-first indexing checker: it fetches any URL as Googlebot Smartphone and Googlebot Desktop and compares 14 signals side by side — title, description, H1, canonical, word count, links, images and structured data — with a parity score out of 100.

Anything that exists only on desktop is invisible to Google. Fix the differing rows first, then re-run the check on the same URL to confirm the fix.

Frequently asked questions

Does mobile-first indexing mean my site must look identical on mobile and desktop?

No — it means the content and signals must be equivalent. Layouts can differ; missing content cannot.

What if I have a separate mobile site (m.example.com)?

Then parity is your job: the mobile site must carry the same content, structured data and tags as the desktop one, or Google will rank the thinner version.

How do I see what Googlebot Smartphone sees?

Search Console → URL Inspection → Test live URL → View tested page shows the rendered HTML and a screenshot from Google's own crawler.
