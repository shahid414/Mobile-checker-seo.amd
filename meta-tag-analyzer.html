Which meta tags matter most?

The title tag is the most important: keep it under 60 characters and put the main keyword near the front. The meta description should be 120–160 characters — it is what searchers read before they click. The canonical tag tells Google which URL is the real one when pages look alike.

Open Graph and Twitter Card tags decide how your page looks when shared on social media. Without og:image, a shared link shows no preview image — and gets far fewer clicks.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good title length?

Under 60 characters. Longer titles get cut off in Google's results.

What is a good description length?

120–160 characters. Shorter descriptions leave space unused; longer ones get cut off.

Do I need Open Graph tags?

Yes, if anyone shares your pages. They control the title, description and image in the share preview.
