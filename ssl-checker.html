Why does SSL matter?

HTTPS encrypts the connection between a visitor and a site, and browsers mark plain HTTP pages as Not Secure. Google also uses HTTPS as a ranking signal, so a missing or expired certificate costs both trust and traffic.

Certificates expire — usually after 90 days for free ones and a year for paid ones. This checker reads the expiry date from public certificate logs, so you can renew before visitors ever see a warning.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when a certificate expires?

Browsers show a full-page security warning and most visitors leave immediately. Renew before the expiry date.

How often should I check?

Once a month is enough for most sites — or set a reminder 14 days before the expiry date this tool shows.

What if the site has no HTTPS?

The checker will tell you straight away: no certificate exists because the site does not use HTTPS.
