How to Check If a Website Is Mobile Friendly

Google's free Mobile-Friendly Test is gone since December 2023. These four free methods replace it — from a 10-second check to a full audit on a real phone.

Try it on your own site — free, no signup.

Mobile SEO Checker →

Method 1: Use a free mobile friendly test

The fastest way: paste the URL into our free mobile friendly test. Our server fetches the page as a phone browser and checks the viewport tag, responsive CSS, font sizes and layout risks, then gives a score out of 100 with the fix for each failing check.

Start here for a quick answer, then use the methods below when you need to see the problem with your own eyes.

Method 2: PageSpeed Insights

Go to pagespeed.web.dev, paste a public URL and stay on the Mobile tab. You get a performance score, Core Web Vitals and a list of opportunities — the same Lighthouse data Google uses. It takes 20–40 seconds because Google runs the test on its own servers.

Our website speed test runs the same check and explains the results in plain language.

Method 3: Chrome DevTools device mode

On a computer, open the page in Chrome, right-click, choose Inspect, then click the phone-and-tablet icon. Device mode shows your page at common phone widths and can slow the connection to mimic a weak signal. It is the best way to spot overflow, layout shifts and menu bugs.

Check at 360 and 390 pixels wide — the most common phone widths — and scroll the whole page. Anything that forces sideways scrolling is a fail.

Method 4: A real phone

Open the site on your own phone the way a stranger would: find a service, tap the phone number, fill out the contact form. Can you read the text without zooming? Does a pop-up cover the page before you can read anything? Does the form work with a touch keyboard?

No tool replaces this. Do it on at least two browsers — Chrome and Safari — before every launch.

Frequently asked questions

What is the quickest free check?

Our mobile friendly test — paste a URL and get a verdict in seconds. PageSpeed Insights takes 20–40 seconds but adds speed data.

What are the most common mobile problems?

A missing viewport tag, text too small to read, tap targets too close together, content wider than the screen, and pop-ups that cover the page.

How often should I check?

After every design change and new page template, plus a monthly check of your top pages.
