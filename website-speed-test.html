What is a good page speed score?

Lighthouse gives a performance score from 0 to 100: 90–100 is good, 50–89 needs improvement, below 50 is poor. The score is a lab measurement — it also shows Core Web Vitals, the metrics Google uses for ranking: LCP should be 2.5 seconds or less, INP 200 ms or less, and CLS 0.1 or less.

The biggest wins are usually the same: compress the largest image and serve it at the right size, remove unused JavaScript, and give images width and height so the layout does not shift while loading.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the test take 20–40 seconds?

Google runs a full Lighthouse audit on its own servers with a mid-range phone profile and a slow mobile network. That is what makes the result trustworthy.

What is the difference between lab and field data?

Lab data is one Lighthouse run. Field data comes from real Chrome users over 28 days. When they disagree, trust the field data — that is the experience Google evaluates.

Does speed affect Google rankings?

Yes — Core Web Vitals are part of Google's ranking systems. But good scores do not guarantee top positions; the most relevant content can still rank with weak speed.
