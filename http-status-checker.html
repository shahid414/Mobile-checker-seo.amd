What do HTTP status codes mean?

A 200 means the page loaded fine. A 301 is a permanent redirect and 302 is temporary — both send visitors and search engines to a new URL. A 404 means the page does not exist, and a 500 means the server failed.

Chains matter: every extra redirect hop slows the page and leaks ranking value. A chain of three or more redirects should be shortened to a single 301 from the old URL straight to the final one.

Frequently asked questions

What is a redirect loop?

When URL A redirects to B and B redirects back to A — the browser gives up and shows an error. This checker flags loops immediately.

301 or 302 — which should I use?

301 for permanent moves, 302 for temporary ones. A 301 passes nearly all ranking value; a 302 passes less.

How many redirects are too many?

More than one hop is wasteful. Google follows up to 10, but users and crawlers both prefer a single direct redirect.
